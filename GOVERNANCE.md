# Governance

## Roles

- **Owners** manage the organization, membership, and settings.
- **Maintainers** review and merge changes and triage issues in their repositories.
- **Contributors** anyone who submits issues, code, or documentation.

## Decision making

Decisions are made by consensus among maintainers of the affected repository. If consensus can't be reached, organization owners make the final call.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of high-quality contributions may be nominated by an existing maintainer and approved by the organization owners.
